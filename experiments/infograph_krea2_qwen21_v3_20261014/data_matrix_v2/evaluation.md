## Summary

36/36 images completed across 12 new cases and three BF16 diffusion models. The cases use six Chinese and six English briefs and add twelve structures/styles beyond v1. Eleven datasets are synthetic fixtures; only the global electricity-access line uses sourced data.

| Model condition | Data/chart mapping | Text/label fidelity | Layout / art direction | Chinese rendering |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Qwen-Image-2.1 BF16 + official T2I PE | 9/10 | 9/10 | 9/10 | 9/10 |
| Krea 2 RAW BF16, natural-language quoted labels, no `text` negative | 3/10 | 2/10 | 6/10 | 2/10 |
| FLUX.2-klein 9B BF16, structured quoted labels | 4/10 | 2/10 | 7/10 | 2/10 |

These are subjective 1–10 scores. Qwen preserved the primary values and chart relationships across the new chart forms in this visual pass. Krea produced attractive individual art directions, but frequent pseudo-text, wrong axes, duplicated/missing labels, or malformed pairings made the results unsuitable as faithful data graphics. FLUX had strong visual styling but often changed axis scale, labels, or coordinates. A correct isolated value does not count as a correct chart mapping.
